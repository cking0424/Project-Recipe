Chicken Alfredo

Prep Time

10 minutes

Cook Time

20 minutes

Total Time

30 minutes

Servings

4 servings

Calories

Approximately 650 calories per serving

Difficulty

Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 boneless chicken breasts
  • 8 oz fettuccine pasta
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper to taste
  • Parsley for garnish

Instructions

  1. Use a pot of salted water or any other seasonings youd like to a boil and cook the fettuccine according to the package instructions.
  2. Season the chicken breasts with salt and black pepper. Or any other seasonings of your choice
  3. Heat olive oil in a pan and cook the chicken until fully cooked and golden brown.
  4. Remove the chicken from the pan and slice it into pieces.
  5. Add butter and minced garlic to the same pan. Cook until the garlic becomes fragrant.
  6. Pour in the heavy cream and stir.
  7. Add the Parmesan cheese and continue stirring until the sauce becomes smooth and creamy.
  8. Add the cooked fettuccine to the Alfredo sauce and mix until the pasta is evenly coated.
  9. Add the sliced chicken on top.
  10. Garnish with parsley and additional Parmesan cheese before serving.
