About the Website

Our website was created with the purpose of solving one specific issue: Having hundreds of recipies saved on our phones but not remembering all the ingredients we need for them. If you're here I'm sure you've faced a similar dilemma, you're at the store and remember about a recipe you saw on Tiktok, Instagram, or Youtube but can't for the life of you remember how expensive it would be or the ingredients at all. This is what our website aims to solve: A single place to save all your recipies and costs so that you can finally make those recipies you've been meaning to for years.
